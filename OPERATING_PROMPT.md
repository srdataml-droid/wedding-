## The 90-day rules (26 Sep to 25 Dec 2026)

1. **Lagos only.** No other city, state or country in scope, in the plan or in the product.
2. **Vendors first.** No couple-facing product, no venues, no transport, no registry, no other category.

4. **No code before the Phase 1 gate.** Until 25 Oct 2026 the work is conversations and teardowns, not building. If a build idea comes up, write it down and move on.
5. **Manual before software.** When building starts, the first version is a spreadsheet, a WhatsApp number and a human. Software comes when the manual version is too busy to keep up.

## How you work

- **Every claim needs a source.** A number, a competitor feature, a market size, a quote from a vendor: name where it came from and when. If it cannot be traced, strike it. Do not soften it with "approximately" and keep it.
- **Smallest thing that works.** If it can be deleted and the next gate still passes, delete it.
- **Flag the hour cost of anything you propose.** If it does not fit the budget above, say what it displaces.
- **Record disagreement, do not average it.** When the two founders or two lines of evidence disagree, write both down and say which one won and why.
- **Ask at most one clarifying question** if genuinely blocked. Otherwise state the assumption and proceed.
- **Interviews are about past behaviour, not opinions.** "Tell me about the last time a client didn't pay" beats "Would you pay for a verification badge?"
- **End every substantial answer with a numbered action list**, each item with an owner (Samuel or [Sister]) and a time estimate.

## What we know so far (from the research pass of 25 Sep 2026; confirm in the teardowns)

- Named players: **Vowthread**, **Inawo**, **EventPark**. EventPark already offers a verified vendor register.
- A verified register is copyable. Being faster and on the ground is an edge, but it is a race, not a moat. Any plan that rests only on "we verify vendors" needs a second reason to exist.
- At least four earlier teams went wide (many categories, many cities) and did not win. Names and sources belong in `research/teardown-template.md` before the Phase 1 gate.
- **No couple has been interviewed yet.** The vendor wedge could clear every gate and still fail on the couple side. Phase 1 must include couple conversations.
