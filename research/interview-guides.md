# Interview Guides: Vendors and Couples

Rules for both guides:

- Ask about the **last time** something happened, not whether they **would** do something. Past behaviour is evidence. Opinions about the future are not.
- Do not pitch. If they ask what we are building, say "we are trying to understand how weddings get organised in Lagos" and return to the question.
- Write down **verbatim quotes**. A quote in their words is worth more than a summary in ours.
- 20 to 30 minutes. Stop on time.
- Fill in the note template the same day.

## A. Vendor guide (owner: [Sister])

Target for Phase 1: 10 vendors across at least 3 categories (for example photographers, caterers, decorators, MCs, makeup, planners). Prioritise vendors who have done at least 10 weddings.

**Opening (2 min)**
- What do you do, and how long have you been doing weddings in Lagos?
- Roughly how many weddings did you work in the last 12 months?

**How clients find them (5 min)**
- Tell me about the last three clients you booked. How did each one find you?
- Where do you spend money or time to be found? Instagram, referrals, planners, platforms?
- Have you ever listed on a platform (Vowthread, Inawo, EventPark, any other)? What happened?

**Where money and time leak (10 min)**
- Tell me about the last time a client did not pay the balance, or paid very late. What did that cost you?
- Tell me about the last time a booking fell through after you had turned down other work.
- Tell me about the last enquiry that turned out to be a waste of time. How often does that happen?
- Has a client ever chosen someone else because they trusted that person more? How did that person earn the trust?
- What do you do today to prove you are legitimate to a new client?

**Money (5 min)**
- What do you pay for today to get or protect business? Ads, a planner's commission, a listing, a lawyer for contracts?
- If we are talking about the leak you described, what would fixing it be worth to you per month? Ask for a number. Do not suggest one.

**Close (3 min)**
- Who else should I talk to?
- Can I come back to you when we have something to show?

## B. Couple guide (owner: either founder)

Target for Phase 1: 5 couples, married in the last 18 months or actively planning. Either partner is fine.

**Opening (2 min)**
- When was or is the wedding, and roughly how many guests?
- Who did most of the vendor-finding, you or a planner?

**Finding vendors (8 min)**
- Walk me through how you found your photographer. Then your caterer. Then one more.
- Did you use any website or app? Which, and what happened?
- How did you decide a vendor could be trusted before paying a deposit?

**What went wrong (10 min)**
- Tell me about the vendor experience you would warn a friend about.
- Did any vendor take a deposit and underdeliver, disappear, or change terms? What did you do?
- Was there a moment you wished someone could vouch for a vendor? What did you do instead?

**Money (5 min)**
- What did you pay in deposits before the wedding, roughly, and how many vendors?
- Did you pay anyone for help choosing or checking vendors? A planner, a friend, a platform?

**Close (3 min)**
- Who else recently married should I talk to?
