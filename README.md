# Together (working name)

The trusted layer for Nigerian weddings, starting with vendors in Lagos.

Two founders. Samuel builds (engineering, Lagos, full-time job, hard cap of about 6 hours a week on this). [Sister] runs the market side (vendor relationships, interviews, in-person checks).

## The ruling that shapes everything in this repo

For the first 90 days (26 Sep to 25 Dec 2026):

- **Lagos only.**
- **Vendors first.**
- **One paid transaction before any new category.**

"Nigeria, then Africa" is the vision sentence. It is not in the plan. The objection to this narrow start, and why it was overruled, is in `DECISIONS.md` (D-001).
